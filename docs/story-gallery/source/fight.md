## A fight, as the two squid recorded it

Recorded in a separate two-tank session: **Ziggy** (adventurous) and **Inky**
(timid, blue), both new hatchlings, linked by friend code and left alone.
Nothing was nudged. Every number and quotation is from the two squids' own
records. The full account is in [fight/README.md](fight/README.md).

|---|---|---|
| 0.9 s into the fight | 1.7 s | 2.5 s |

Inky's tank, eight minutes in, with the cloud over the two of them.

### What led up to it

| When | Who | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| 0:10 | Ziggy | Swam into Inky's tank ten seconds after the tanks were linked. |
| 0:15 | Ziggy | Grew `social_play`: its way of meeting a squid is to go up to it. |
| 2:02 | Inky | Grew `social_flee`: its way is to get away. |
| 3:10 | Inky | Filed the visit so far as a bad encounter with Ziggy (felt -12.2): "I am warier of squid, even ones I do not know - and of other aquariums." |
| 3:18 | Inky | Grew `recognises_squid` and `remembers_bad_encounter`. |
| 7:20 to 8:00 | both | Ziggy kept exploring while its anxiety climbed from 56 to 61. Inky was at anxiety 100, fleeing throughout. |
| 8:00 | Ziggy | Grew `aggression`: "I went at another squid and had no neuron for that." |

### What started the fight

Ziggy did: **"I went for Inky: my aggression was 100, past the 50 it takes to
start a fight."** The aggression neuron was four seconds old. It grew because
Ziggy approached another squid, as it had all visit, while its own anxiety
was high: approaching while calm is play, approaching while anxious is going
at another squid. Once grown the neuron went straight to 100.

Nothing Inky did provoked it and nothing was being contested. Inky's record
of the same moment: **"Ziggy came at me. I fought back with my anxiety at
100."**

### Why Ziggy lost

| | Ziggy (attacker) | Inky (defender) |
|---|---|---|
| Body condition | 0.66 | 0.70 |
| Commitment | 100, from aggression | 100, from anxiety |
| **Ability** | **99** | **105** |
| Chance of winning | 47% | 53% |

Both committed completely, so that decided nothing. The difference was the
body: Ziggy was slightly hungrier (58 against 54) and more tired (79 against
77) after eight minutes of exploring. It was close to a coin toss tilted
Inky's way, and it fell Inky's way. Thirty-four seconds later Ziggy attacked
again, now hurt: ability 90 against 106, a 42% chance. It lost again.

### What it cost

| | Ziggy (lost twice) | Inky (won twice) |
|---|---|---|
| Health, per fight | -8 | -3 |
| Happiness | -10 | 0 |
| Anxiety | +15 | -5 |
| Satisfaction | -5 | 0 |

### What each was left with

**Ziggy**

- Remembers both: "Lost a fight it started (aggression 100): ability 99
  against 105, so it had a 47% chance", then 90 against 106 at 42%.
- Filed the whole visit as a **beaten encounter with Inky**, the worst memory
  of the session (felt -84.8, fights lost 2).
- "Something has changed in me: my aggression rests at 4 now, not 0, and
  always will - I lost a fight with Inky." After the second: 8. It never goes
  back down.
- Still "drawn to other squid", though warier. Three seconds after getting
  home it went back to Inky's tank.

**Inky**

- Remembers both as wins "when it was attacked".
- Winning did not make the visit a good one: its memory of Ziggy is still a
  bad encounter.
- Grew no aggression. It never started anything; both times it fought because
  it was attacked, with its anxiety at 100.
- Ended "afraid of other squid".

twice found it did not: Ziggy kept coming.
