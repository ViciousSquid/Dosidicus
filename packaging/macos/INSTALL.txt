Drag Dosidicus.app onto Applications, then launch Dosidicus from Applications.

The app supports Intel and Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 11 or later.
Python and Qt are bundled; no separate Python installation is required.

Saves, settings, memories, logs and exports are stored in:
~/Library/Application Support/Dosidicus/

This development build is ad-hoc signed and is not Apple-notarized.
macOS may require approval in System Settings > Privacy & Security.
