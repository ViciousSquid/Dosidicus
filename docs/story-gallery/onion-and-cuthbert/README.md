# Onion went visiting. Cuthbert threw rocks.

On 11 November 2026, **Onion** visited **Cuthbert** over the public internet in
Dosidicus 3.5.0. They were in different cities. Cuthbert's caretaker watched
the encounter; Onion's caretaker watched Onion leave, then saw an empty tank
with its decorations still in place.

Onion came home with a bad memory of Cuthbert. Cuthbert remembered having fun
throwing rocks.

This is a player-reported encounter supported by Cuthbert's supplied save and
a screenshot of Onion's memory report. It is separate from the gallery's
four-tank demonstration, which used a private network on one machine.

## The visit nobody at home could see

Onion's caretaker had named and raised him. When Onion left, there was no
live view of the other tank on that screen. The caretaker's girlfriend, in
the other city, saw what happened in Cuthbert's tank and described it
afterwards. Onion's own memory report supplied another account when he came
home.

> “I only saw an empty tank with decorations in it, I only saw Onion leave.”

The absence mattered: Onion had an experience outside his caretaker's view,
and returned with consequences that could be inspected.

## Two rocks, no fight

Eyewitness account from Cuthbert's owner:

> Onion entered the tank and was out of sight from Cuthbert initially. I resized the tank as onion entered and after doing so Cuthbert inked. He then saw Onion for the first time and immediately became aggressive and curious. He hid in his plants then when onion ventured closer to him Cuthbert picked up a rock and threw it at Onion, he continued to do this on and off until Onion left.

Cuthbert's save corroborates **two hits given to Onion**, with **zero fights
won and zero fights lost** in the rock-related encounter record. Onion's
report independently says **two rock hits received from Cuthbert**. Rock
damage and a recorded fight are distinct events here.

These are each squid's own records, not a synchronised transcript. Their
durations and values describe different recorded windows and should not be
combined into one exact visit length or a claim of multiple visits.

## What grew in Cuthbert

His saved provenance gives the reason for growing `aggression`:

> “It went at another squid while its anxiety was high and had no neuron for that”

The recorded creation evidence is `can_see_squid = 100`, `act_play = 38`, and
`anxiety = 84.2`. The new representation was wired into movement and play.
The save also records the growth of `social_play`, `recognises_squid`,
`remembers_good_encounter`, and `remembers_bad_encounter`.

His rock-throwing memory says **“Had fun throwing a rock!”**, marks the
activity positive, and records effects of happiness **+9**, satisfaction
**+11**, and anxiety **−9**. Its throw metadata names **Onion** as the target
and `act_play` as the driving action. This records a rewarding throw, not
proof that the squid understood another's suffering or deliberately chose
cruelty. Nor does that throw entry alone prove a hit; the social record
supplies the two hits.

Cuthbert's caretaker described him as a bully with permanent aggression of
60. That is a player interpretation and observation, **not a confirmed
permanent baseline in this archive**: the saved activation is about **93.13**,
while the aggression neuron's `resting_level` is `null` and its
`resting_history` is empty. A current activation and an enduring resting
level are different measurements.

## The same meeting, different histories

Onion's report names Cuthbert and remembers the encounter badly. Cuthbert
has a positive rock-play memory and a later positive social record of Onion.
He also retains a negative rock-related record. His history is more mixed
than simply “Cuthbert enjoyed everything.”

The two squid did not acquire a shared verdict. Each retained consequences
from its own side of the encounter. For the players, those records made an
unseen visit reconstructable: one person witnessed it, another waited beside
an empty tank, and the visitor came home with a history.

## Sources and limits

- **Caretaker account:** the public-internet connection between different
  cities, who witnessed the visit, Onion's departure and flight, and the lack
  of a revisit at the time of reporting.
- **Onion's displayed memory report:** “Bad encounter with Cuthbert”, value
  −45.2, 69 seconds, two rock hits, one encounter, and named recognition.
  Onion's full save was not supplied for this entry.
- **Cuthbert's supplied save:** `game_state.json`, `brain_state.json`,
  `LongTerm.json`, `statistics.json`, and `timeline.json`. Selected verbatim
  fields, archive SHA-256, and JSON locations are in
  [evidence.json](evidence.json). The full save is not published here.
